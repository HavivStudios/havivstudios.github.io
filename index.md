# Gloamreach

**An idle RPG at the edge of the dark.** The hero keeps working while you are away.

Gloamreach is a dark-fantasy idle RPG for Android by HavivStudios. Pick a class, point the
hero at a zone, and come back to find the gold piled up and the levels landed. No energy
meter, no loot boxes, one kind of advert you choose to watch, and no account of its own.

Coming to Google Play. The listing link will appear here when the game is live.
